Wow Fact TV Uploader

A private publishing tool used by the owner of the Wow Fact TV YouTube channel to upload the channel's own illustrated story videos. It is not offered to the public and serves exactly one channel.

About Privacy Policy Terms of Service Watch on YouTube

About

Wow Fact TV publishes short illustrated stories with a good message: friendship, protecting nature, respect for elders and for other people's work. Stories are produced in four languages (Azerbaijani, Turkish, English and Russian). Each video shows its target age, and stories for children and for adults are kept in separate playlists.

Channel: youtube.com/@WOWFACTTV-h9x.

The uploader runs on the channel owner's personal computer in the n8n automation tool. It prepares each video and uses the YouTube Data API to upload it to the owner's own channel.

WriteA story script is drafted.
ProduceNarration, illustrations and music are combined into a video.
ReviewThe owner receives the video and its title, description and tags.
UploadThe video is uploaded to the owner's channel via the YouTube Data API.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Wow Fact TV Uploader ("the app") handles information. The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who uses the app

The app has a single user: the owner of the Wow Fact TV channel. It does not offer sign-up to anyone else and does not access other people's YouTube accounts.

Information the app accesses

  • Through YouTube API Services, with the owner's permission, the app uploads videos and sets their title, description, tags, privacy status and playlist on the owner's own channel.
  • The app does not read viewers' personal data, comments, subscriber lists or analytics.

How information is stored

  • The access token Google issues after the owner grants permission is stored only on the owner's personal computer, inside the local n8n installation. It is not sent to any server operated by the app.
  • Video files and their texts are stored on the owner's computer and on YouTube.
  • The app does not use cookies and does not collect device or location data.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Data goes only to YouTube, as needed to upload videos to the owner's channel.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google account connections page (also reachable at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the local n8n installation within 7 days. Any data obtained through YouTube API Services is not kept for longer than 30 days unless it is refreshed.
  • Videos already uploaded remain on the channel and can be deleted in YouTube Studio.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
